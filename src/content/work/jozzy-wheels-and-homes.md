## The challenge

The business ran on WhatsApp messages and memory. Requests got lost, details were missing and nobody could see what was still open.

## What I did

I designed and shipped a full-stack site that turns every enquiry into a structured request:

- **Smart request forms** — validated details and a readable reference number for every request.
- **Instant hand-off** — an email alert to the team and a pre-filled WhatsApp message to the customer.
- **Admin dashboard** — a simple place for non-technical staff to see, update and close requests, with secure sign-in and roles.

I led product, design and architecture, and built it with an AI coding agent on a free-tier stack.
