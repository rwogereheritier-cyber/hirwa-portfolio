## The challenge

Orphictek builds enterprise technology for African businesses, but its website looked like a company from another decade. It needed to feel as reliable as the systems it sells.

## The website

I rebuilt the site from the ground up — clearer services, a modern layout and a structure that tells enterprise buyers what Orphictek does in seconds.

## ORPHIC54

The company is growing into ORPHIC54 — named for Africa's 54 nations. I designed the identity with a systems-first, editorial direction and no decorative or tribal clichés: a precise mark, a tight type system and a 12-column grid, documented in a 16-page brand book.

## Where it stands

The website is live at orphictek.com. The ORPHIC54 identity is ready for rollout.
